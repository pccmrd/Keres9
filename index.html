KERES-9

CATALOG ZERO · LUNAR ORBIT · 2035

Haul engines drag rocks and comets in from the Belt and the Kuiper dark and park them in lunar orbit. Then the KERES-9 catalog AI died, and nobody knows what is up there anymore. You live in a hab on the Shackleton rim. Every morning you ride the cable to orbit. Start on a crew with a suit and a cutter. Buy a tug. Then buy a lander and fly it all the way down to the pad outside your door.

TOUCH: left stick moves/RCS (screen-relative) · right stick turns · right rail is throttle · tap to target

THE CAREER

1 · CREW HANDWalk from HAB 14 to the SELENE LIFT, ride the cable to SELENE HIGH, sign on with Foreman Lu in the CREW OFFICE. The MULE-3 shuttle drops you at a rock: cut ore on EVA, fill your sack, get back aboard before the horn. Pay = base + half your haul.
2 · ORBITAL TUGBuy a WREN at the SELENE HIGH yard. It flies anywhere in the band. Harpoon a rock and mine from the hull. A bigger hold means more money.
3 · LANDERBuy a HARE at SHACKLETON DEPOT on the surface. You fly it all the way: launch from the pad, climb, circularise, mine and trade in orbit, then deorbit and land. Ice and metal sell for much more on the ground.

FLYING NEAR THE MOON

GravityThe Moon pulls 1.6 m/s² at the surface. The camera rotates so "up" is always away from the Moon. The dotted line is your ballistic path, and IMPACT means it hits the ground.
Orbit bandRocks and stations sit near the synchronous radius, where gravity and the frame balance out. They hang still in the sky above the Rim, which is why the cable works.
Throttle / sticksRight rail is the main engine. The left stick fires RCS relative to the screen, and the right stick turns. SAS (V in flight) holds UP, RETRO or PRO.
MATCH / BRAKEMATCH holds the target's velocity. BRAKE kills your velocity against the band. Both hover-compensate half of gravity.
LandingThe HARE is a tail-sitter. Come down nose-up, under 3 m/s, with less than 20° of tilt, and the pad clamps you automatically. You can also put down on flat regolith.

DISCOVERY

SurveyUncatalogued rocks show as UNKNOWN. Keep one inside your array range (or ping it with your suit scanner) until the survey finishes. The Catalog Office pays a bounty for every body.
Deep contactsC opens the directional array. Sweep it over a rock and tune the dial onto a resonance, then hold the lock to reveal veins, sealed chambers, KERES-9 core shards and claim beacons.

EVA

WalkingOn the ground: A/D walk, W hop. Air refills indoors. Stations and habs have rooms. Walk into a room and press F at its terminal.
In orbitThe suit jetpack is in the left stick, and M runs your cutter. Ore goes straight into your sack. Bring it to the MULE or your own ship.

AI PLAYER

AI / PThe computer plays with the same sticks, rail and buttons you use. It walks, rides the lift, works crew shifts, buys a tug, mines, buys a lander, launches, lands and delivers. Touch the controls to take over.
